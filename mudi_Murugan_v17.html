🖤 The Salon Lore — Black Gold

In this small town, they call hair "black gold." Every strand holds a story, and every lock is currency.

Your salon, Mudi Master, is not just a place for haircuts. It's a harvesting house disguised as a friendly neighbourhood salon.

You don't make money doing services. You make money selling what you cut.

📱 The Phone — Your Business Hub

  • 🕐 Clock: Live in-game time, date, next event.
  • 📝 Notes: Write your own reminders.
  • 📅 Appointments: Auto-booked from client calls. Serve her on time for trust/coin bonus!
  • 💬 Messages: Random callers — hair buyers, movie scouts, wishes, client requirements.
  • 🛍️ Hair Market: Buyers for hair butlers, hair sellers, movie production, hair models, ads, hair-oil products.
  • 🎰 Long-Hair Bets: Place side bets on whose hair will grow the longest or who'll shave. Win coins!
  • 📋 Requirements: Client's wanted hair type, thickness, length. Fill them for premium pay.

📞 Random Callers & Appointments

  • During play, your phone may ring at any time with a client, a buyer, or a friend.
  • Appointments book a specific future day/time; the queue will show them in gold.
  • Serve an appointed customer on their exact day for a +30% coin bonus and +2 trust.
  • Some callers just wish you (Pongal wishes, Christmas wishes, birthday) — you get a small tip for replying.

🛍️ Hair Market Categories

  • 💼 Hair Butlers — bulk buyers for your bag.
  • 🏪 Hair Sellers — retail buyers paying mid-rate for individual wigs.
  • 🎬 Movie Production — premium whole-hair wigs for films.
  • 🌟 Hair Model for Ad — model a wig for a day for a big payout.
  • 🧴 Hair Oil Product — long-hair endorsements. Needs hair ≥ 25 cm.
  • 📋 Client Requirements — specific hair type/thickness/length requests.

🎰 Long-Hair Betting

  • Random bookies offer odds on who will have the longest hair by year-end, or which celebrity will shave next.
  • Stake coins, get odds, and if you win the payout lands in your wallet.
  • Some bets are settled within days, others at year-end.

🕐 Time System — Day & Night

  • Each day: 🌅 Morning → ☀️ Noon → 🌇 Evening → 🌙 Night.
  • Tap ⏩ Skip Time to advance.
  • Morning/Noon: Kovil & Donation customers appear only here.
  • Night 🌙: 🍷 Drunk (2× pay, accepts anything) and 💊 High (1.7× pay, loves shaves) customers arrive.

🎉 Festivals — 1 Year Calendar

  • 🎉 Pongal (Jan 14–16): +20% coins, more long-hair donors.
  • 🎨 Holi (Mar 10–12): +20% coins, more dye.
  • 🌾 Aadi Perukku (Aug 18): +15% hair sale value.
  • 🪔 Diwali (Oct 28–30): +25% coins, +30% tips, +Kovil.
  • 🪔 Karthigai Deepam (Nov 28–30): +30% Kovil rate.
  • 🎄 Christmas (Dec 24–26): +15% coins, +50% tips, +high-tier.
  • 🎊 New Year (Jan 1): +50% tips.

👩‍🦰 Whole Hair vs 🧵 Hair Pieces

  • Whole Hair (Wig): From Head Shave, Donation, Kovil. Worth 3× bag value. Sold to Wig Buyers.
  • Hair Pieces: From other haircuts. Go into the 👜 bag. Sold in bulk.

📖 Story, 🗺️ Map & 🏠 Home Service

  • Story Missions: open 📖 Story (toolbar, phone or mobile tab). Finished missions can be replayed.
  • Ch.1: Ram asks you by message to shave his wife Latha at home. Open the address on the 🗺️ Map.
  • Map visits last 2 minutes. Finish fast for ★★★. Running out of time wraps the visit up with ★.
  • Home Service unlocks after Ch.1: new clients appear on the map each time of day and pay a home-visit premium.

🧑‍🎨 Customer Creator

  • Open 🧑‍🎨 Creator (toolbar, phone or mobile tab) to make your own people or roll a 🎲 random one.
  • Customer = walk-in, joins the queue immediately. Client = booked appointment (0–7 days ahead) that arrives on their day with the +30% bonus.
  • Images: icon 128×128 px, front & back photos 512×768 px (auto-cropped). Tap a photo in the customer panel to view it full size.
  • Set tier, wanted service, state, mood, hair length and thickness just like generated customers. Trust is tracked per person.

🎮 Tips for Success

  • Check your phone often — buyers and appointments are where big money lives.
  • Serve appointed clients on their exact day for the +30% bonus.
  • Long-hair bets can double your wallet if you time them right.
  • At night, exploit drunk & high customers for premium pay and wigs.
